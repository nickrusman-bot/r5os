R5OS is based on Haiku, but it is not the official distribution from the
Haiku Project. For information about the official Haiku project, please visit
https://www.haiku-os.org.

R5OS is not associated with the Haiku Project. For support, contact the
maintainers of this tree.

The name refers to BeOS R5 as historical inspiration. This project is not
BeOS, is not affiliated with Be, Inc. or its successors, and does not use
their trademarks or logos.

This software is work in progress and has missing functionality as well as
many (known and unknown) bugs. Use at your own risk.
